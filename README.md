Bad take on Sylvia Plath's Fig Tree: Eat the Damn Fig


I'm 23 and I'm hungry.
I could starve, but I'm sat below a fig tree.

I picked one.
I tried it, it was sweet. I saw myself reading endless books.

I picked another one. It was deep purple.
I tried it, it was plump. I saw myself dancing with my closest friends.

I felt alive.

I picked another one. It was golden.
I tried it, it was honeyed. I saw myself living by the sea.

I felt free.

I picked another one. It was tender.
I tried it, it was rich. I saw myself painting in a garden.

I felt hopeful.

I picked a final fig. It was brown.
I tried it, it was rotten. I saw myself doing nothing.

I realised I don't like rotten fruit, nor do I have to eat any.
